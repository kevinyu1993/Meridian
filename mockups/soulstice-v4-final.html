Sunrise photo — what to upload

The CSS sunrise in this mockup is a stand-in. For the real site, upload a photographic sunrise that matches the mood. Squarespace's image uploader has a free Unsplash search built in — type the queries below.

SearchWhat to look for
sunrise mountains Pink/orange sky with mountain or hill silhouette. Strong horizon, lots of color. Most photographic option — feels cinematic.
sunrise sky pink purple Pure-sky shots, no horizon. Pure color study, more abstract. Works because your overlay is the bottom 30% anyway.
sunrise calm water Sky reflected in still water. Doubles the color. Tends to be quieter than mountain shots.
golden hour window Interior shot with warm light pouring through a window. Less literal "sunrise," more "the feeling of one." Most on-brand if you want something quieter than a landscape.
palm sunrise pink Pink/lavender sky with palm silhouette — close to your existing brand-book sunset reference (slide 12). Good fidelity move.

What to avoid

  • Photos with people. The CTA "Meet our therapists" already handles people. Hero should be mood, not subjects.
  • Photos with strong center subjects. Your text is centered. Anything visually centered in the photo will fight the type. Pick photos with empty middle or low horizons.
  • Photos that read green/blue/cool. The brand is sunrise — warm. Cool tones will fight the lavender CTA and the peach eyebrow.
  • Photos that are too literal. A clock at 6am, a coffee cup with steam, etc. — these read as stock. Abstract sunrises do more work.
